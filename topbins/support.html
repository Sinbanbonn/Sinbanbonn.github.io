Support

Need help with Top Bins: Penalty & Free Kick? Check the questions below. If you still need help, just email us.

How do I restore Remove Ads?

Open the game and go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID (iPhone) or Google account (Android) you used to buy.

I bought coins but did not get them

Close and reopen the game while online. If the coins still do not appear, email us with the date of the purchase and the order number from your receipt.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use the Order history in the Google Play app.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Scores, coins and unlocked items are stored on your device and do not move automatically. Remove Ads can be restored on a new device with Restore Purchases.

How do I change my ad consent?

In the game, open Settings → Privacy Settings.
