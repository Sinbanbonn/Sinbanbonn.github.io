TOP BINS

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This policy explains what data the game Top Bins: Penalty & Free Kick ("the game") collects on iOS and Android, why, and what choices you have.

Who we are

Top Bins is made by an independent developer, DANL Studio. If you have any questions about privacy, contact us at danliosteam2025@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

The game has no user accounts and we do not run our own servers. Your best scores, coins, unlocked items and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. With your consent, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), device information and data about how you interact with ads to show more relevant ads and measure their performance.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform).
  • On iOS, tracking only happens after you give permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • If you do not give consent, you will see non-personalized ads only.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

We use Firebase Analytics to collect usage statistics such as game events, device type, OS version and approximate country. This helps us understand how the game is played and make it better. Firebase Analytics respects the consent choices you make in the game.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report with the state of the app at the time of the crash, your device model and OS version. We use these reports only to find and fix bugs.

Purchases (Apple, Google Play, RevenueCat)

Purchases such as Remove Ads and coin packs are processed by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play. We never receive your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check that a purchase is valid and to restore it on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase receipts, the product bought, price, currency and country. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

Leaderboards (Game Center, Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Game Center on iOS or Google Play Games on Android, the game may use it for leaderboards. This data is processed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights and choices

  • Ad consent: change it in the game under Settings → Privacy Settings. On iOS also in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • Game progress: delete it by deleting the app.
  • Anything else: email us at danliosteam2025@gmail.com and we will help, including requests to delete data held by our service providers.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date.
