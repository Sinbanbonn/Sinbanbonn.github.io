Idle Delivery Man

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Idle Delivery Man ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app works without accounts. We do not run our own servers for player data; some data is processed by the advertising and purchase services described below.

Information we do not collect

  • You do not need to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Information stored on your device

  • Game progress and settings. Your progress, in-game currency, upgrades, language and sound settings are saved only on your device.
  • Player name. The name you enter in the game is stored only on your device. It appears in your profile and in the local leaderboard and is not sent to us.
  • Reminders. If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device. No push token or notification data is sent to us.

You can delete all of this data at any time by deleting the app.

Advertising

The app shows optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. You choose whether to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, with your permission, or the Android advertising ID);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether they were watched;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information from the ads SDK.

On iOS the app asks for your permission before your device's advertising identifier is used for tracking. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised using that identifier. Where required by law, the app also asks for your consent to personalised advertising. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

In-app purchases

The app offers optional purchases (crystal packs and Gold Pass). Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we never receive your card details. To validate and restore purchases we use RevenueCat, which processes your purchase history for this app together with a random, anonymous app user identifier. This identifier is not linked to your name or contact details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Technical data collected by the game engine

The app is built with the Unity engine. Unity may collect limited, anonymous technical information about the device and app performance, such as device model, operating system version and performance statistics, to keep the engine working well. This data does not identify you personally. See Unity's privacy policy.

Beta testing through TestFlight

If you test a beta version through Apple TestFlight, Apple may share with us information such as your TestFlight email or name (if you joined by invitation), crash reports, usage statistics and feedback you choose to send. We use this only to fix bugs and improve the app. See Apple's privacy policy.

Your choices

  • You can play without watching ads and without making purchases.
  • You can allow or deny tracking in your device settings (iOS: Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking) and reset or limit the advertising ID on Android.
  • To ask about or request deletion of data held by our service providers, write to us at the address below and we will help with your request.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
