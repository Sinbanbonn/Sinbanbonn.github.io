My purchase did not arrive

Use the Restore Purchases button in the game. Gold Pass is restored on any device signed in with the same store account. If crystals are missing, send us the receipt from the store and we will sort it out.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play, so refunds are requested there: reportaproblem.apple.com for Apple, or your order history in Google Play.

Is my progress saved?

Progress is stored only on your device. There is no account or cloud save, so deleting the game also deletes its progress.
