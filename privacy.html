Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This policy explains what data the game Brick Wall: Goalie Reflex ("the game") collects, why, and what choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

Who we are

Brick Wall: Goalie Reflex is made by an independent developer. If you have any questions about privacy, contact us at danliosteam2025@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

The game has no user accounts and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress, statistics and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see or access them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. With your consent, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), device information and data about how you interact with ads to show more relevant ads and measure their performance.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform).
  • On iOS, tracking only happens after you give permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • If you do not give consent, you will see non-personalized ads only.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymized usage statistics, such as game events, device type, OS version and approximate country. This helps us understand how the game is played and make it better. Firebase Analytics respects the consent choices you make in the game.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report. It includes the state of the app at the time of the crash, your device model and OS version. We use these reports only to find and fix bugs.

Game Center (Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game uses it for leaderboards and achievements. This data is processed by Apple under its own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Purchases and subscriptions

All purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights and choices

You can access, delete or withdraw consent for your data at any time:

  • Ad consent: change it in the game under "Privacy Settings", and in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Game progress: delete it by resetting progress in the game settings, or by deleting the app.
  • Anything else: email us at danliosteam2025@gmail.com and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date.
